    I Prologue

    A city that kneels
    to no throne.

    Twenty-six centuries ago this land was ice, and its only city sat inside a wall of storm raised by a god who could not tell obedience from love. A nameless bard, a red-haired knight, an archer and a wind spirit no bigger than a hand decided that was not good enough.

    They won. The spirit took the shape of the bard who did not live to see it, blew the snow off the mountains, and then did the strangest thing a god has ever done with a throne: he declined it. Mondstadt has governed itself ever since, badly at times and bravely at others, and has never once asked him to come back and rule.

    What follows is a walker’s almanac of the place. Read it in any order. The wind does.
